# Engineering note: Q3 platform migration

From Hannah Kowalski, Head of Engineering (CC300 owner). June 2026.

## What changed

Our colocation provider gave notice in May that they will close the facility at the end of 2026. The data-centre exit and platform migration, budgeted for FY2027, has therefore been brought forward to Q3 2026.

## Cost impact

- **Contractors (6100):** we are bringing in a migration team of specialist contractors from July to November. Monthly contractor spend will run at roughly two and a half times budget for that period.
- **Cloud hosting (5000):** old and new platforms will run in parallel during cut-over, so hosting will run around 15% above budget in Q3.

## Approval and funding

The CTO approved the contractor ramp on 18 June. The cost is funded by pulling forward the FY2027 migration budget, so this is a **timing** variance for the group across the two years, though it is adverse against the FY2026 budget.

## Expected end

Contractor spend returns to budget in December once cut-over completes.
