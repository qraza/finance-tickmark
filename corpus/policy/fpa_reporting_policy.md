# FP&A reporting policy

Larkspur Ltd (fictional). Owner: Group FP&A.

## Sign convention

Revenue and costs are both stored as positive amounts. A variance is always **actual minus budget**. For revenue, a positive variance is favourable. For costs (COGS and opex), a positive variance is adverse.

## Materiality

Cost-centre owners must explain any monthly variance on a single account above **£50k or 10% of budget**, whichever is lower. Explanations go into the monthly management commentary.

## Timing versus permanent variances

Every explained variance must be labelled as either:

- **Timing:** spend or income moved between months; the full-year forecast is unchanged.
- **Permanent:** the full-year outcome changes; the forecast must be updated.

## FX

FX translation differences are reported separately from trading. When the dollar moves against the budget rate, the commentary states the constant-currency position.

## Close calendar

Books close on working day 5. Commentary is published on working day 8. Actuals are available through September 2026.
