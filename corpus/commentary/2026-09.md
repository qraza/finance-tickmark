# Management commentary: September 2026

Prepared by Group FP&A, Larkspur Ltd. Reporting currency GBP. Figures are month actuals against the FY2026 budget.

## Headline

Revenue was £2,578k against a budget of £2,606k (-£28k variance). Operating expenses were £1,972k against £1,776k (£196k variance).

## Variance notes

Engineering contractor spend was £150k against a budget of £60k. The data-centre exit and platform migration was brought forward from FY2027 to Q3 after the colocation provider gave notice. The CTO approved the contractor ramp in June; it is funded from the FY2027 migration budget and is expected to end in November. Cloud hosting is also running above budget while old and new platforms run in parallel.

US revenue was £1,040k in GBP against a budget of £1,086k. The shortfall is currency, not trading: the dollar weakened to around 0.751 GBP per USD against the budget rate of 0.79. In constant currency, US revenue is on plan.

Customer Operations costs were £229k against £273k. The hiring freeze announced in July has left four support roles vacant. Service levels are being watched weekly; backfill is planned for Q4 once the freeze lifts.

Marketing programmes were £309k against £165k, as the London customer summit moved from June took place this month. Year to date, marketing programmes are close to budget.
