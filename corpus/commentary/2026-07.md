# Management commentary: July 2026

Prepared by Group FP&A, Larkspur Ltd. Reporting currency GBP. Figures are month actuals against the FY2026 budget.

## Headline

Revenue was £2,481k against a budget of £2,530k (-£49k variance). Operating expenses were £1,827k against £1,776k (£51k variance).

## Variance notes

Engineering contractor spend was £138k against a budget of £60k. The data-centre exit and platform migration was brought forward from FY2027 to Q3 after the colocation provider gave notice. The CTO approved the contractor ramp in June; it is funded from the FY2027 migration budget and is expected to end in November. Cloud hosting is also running above budget while old and new platforms run in parallel.

US revenue was £999k in GBP against a budget of £1,054k. The shortfall is currency, not trading: the dollar weakened to around 0.752 GBP per USD against the budget rate of 0.79. In constant currency, US revenue is on plan.

Customer Operations costs were £229k against £273k. The hiring freeze announced in July has left four support roles and two back-office roles vacant, so both support staff costs and salaries and benefits are below budget. Service levels are being watched weekly; backfill is planned for Q4 once the freeze lifts.
