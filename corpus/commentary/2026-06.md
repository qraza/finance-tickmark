# Management commentary: June 2026

Prepared by Group FP&A, Larkspur Ltd. Reporting currency GBP. Figures are month actuals against the FY2026 budget.

## Headline

Revenue was £2,470k against a budget of £2,493k (-£22k variance). Operating expenses were £1,699k against £1,776k (-£77k variance).

## Variance notes

Marketing programmes were £91k against £165k. The London customer summit has moved from June to September after the venue contract was renegotiated. Summit costs will now land in September; this is a timing difference, not a saving, and the full-year forecast is unchanged.
