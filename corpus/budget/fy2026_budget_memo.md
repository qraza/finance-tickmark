# FY2026 budget memo

Larkspur Ltd (fictional). Approved by the board in December 2025. Owner: Group FP&A.

## Basis of preparation

The FY2026 budget covers January to December 2026 for two entities: Larkspur Ltd (UK01, GBP) and Larkspur Inc (US01, USD). Group reporting currency is GBP.

US figures are budgeted in USD and translated at a single budget rate of **0.79 GBP per USD** for every month. Actuals are translated at the monthly average rate. Differences between the two are reported as FX translation, not trading performance. Constant-currency comparisons restate actuals at the budget rate.

## Revenue

Subscription revenue is budgeted to grow 1.5% month on month in both entities, driven by net new annual contracts in the Commercial cost centre. Professional services revenue follows the same phasing.

## Costs

- **Engineering (CC300):** headcount flat. A small contractor budget covers specialist work. The data-centre exit and platform migration is planned for FY2027 and is not in this budget.
- **Marketing (CC200):** marketing programmes include the London customer summit, phased into June.
- **Customer Operations (CC400):** support headcount grows with the customer base; hiring plan phased evenly.
- **G&A (CC500):** rent and facilities fixed; no office moves planned.

## Phasing

All cost lines are phased evenly across the year except the customer summit (June) and revenue growth (monthly compounding).
